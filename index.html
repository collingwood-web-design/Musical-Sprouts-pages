How to book

Three steps — no account to create and no app to download.

Reserve your spot

Book a time with Kelly on Calendly and we’ll settle which class fits your child. Prefer email? Just write and we’ll hold your spot.

Pay your invoice

A Wave invoice arrives by email within one business day. Pay by credit card or bank transfer — memberships bill automatically on the 1st.

Come and sing

You’ll get a confirmation with the address, what to bring, and what to expect in your first class.

Come sing with us

Fall 2026 starts Thursday September 10 and Friday September 11. Bring a water bottle, comfy clothes, and someone small.
