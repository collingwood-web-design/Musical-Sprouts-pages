How to book

Three steps — no account to create and no app to download.

Reserve your spot

Register for your class on Luma and pick the class and session that fits your child. Prefer to write? Send a note and we’ll hold your spot.

Pay by e-transfer

Payment is by e-transfer to contactmusicalsprouts@gmail.com. We’ll email the amount after you register.

Come and sing

You’ll get a confirmation with the address, what to bring, and what to expect in your first class.

Say hello

Tell us a little about your child and the class you’re curious about. We’ll write back with the next step.

Come sing with us

Bring a water bottle, comfy clothes, and someone small.
