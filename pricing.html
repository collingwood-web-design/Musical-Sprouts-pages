Good to know

The practical details, in plain language.

Is there a registration fee?
No registration fee and no materials fee. The price you see, plus HST, is the price you pay. The one exception is a $35 + HST administrative fee if you withdraw after registering.
What’s the difference between a session and the membership?
A session is 7 classes paid up front, with your spot guaranteed and two make-ups. The membership is $88 billed monthly, covers every class in the month whether there are three or four, lets you move between Thursday and Friday, includes unlimited make-ups, and carries you across the week off into session 2 without re-registering. Across the fall, membership works out to $25.14 a class ($352 total). A session stays at $28 a class — booking both sessions together for $380 is still available if you prefer.
September and December only have three classes. Am I paying the same?
Yes — the membership is a flat monthly rate that averages out to $25.14 a class across the fall, the same way a gym membership does. It’s $29.33 a class in the short months and $22.00 in the longer ones. If you’d rather pay per session instead, $196 covers 7 classes at $28 each.
How do make-up classes work?
Session families get 2 make-ups per session; members get unlimited make-ups. Both are space permitting. Let us know before the class you’ll miss and we’ll find you another spot that week where we can.
Can I switch class times?
Yes, and there’s no fee. Switching to another time within the same age group and session is free, subject to space. Members can move between Thursday and Friday whenever they like.
Can I cancel my membership?
After the first two months it’s month to month — email us 30 days ahead and billing stops at the end of the following cycle. The $35 withdrawal fee doesn’t apply to memberships.
What if we join partway through a session?
Session fees are prorated from your first class, so you only pay for the weeks that are left. Drop us a note and we’ll send an adjusted invoice.
Which class does my child belong in?
Sproutlings is for toddlers 1–3 on Thursdays, Budding Sprouts for preschoolers 3–5 on Thursdays, and Sprouts All-Ages on Fridays welcomes 0–5 together. If your child is right on the edge, come to a trial or book a discovery call and we’ll decide together.
Do you refund unused classes?
Session fees aren’t refundable once classes begin, and a $35 + HST administrative fee applies to withdrawals. In the case of a longer illness or a move we’ll issue a credit toward a future session.
