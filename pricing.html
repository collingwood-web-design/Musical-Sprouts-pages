How the membership works

The membership is the most flexible way to come to Musical Sprouts, and it’s always less per class than a session: $25.14 + HST a class, compared with $28 + HST.

  • Join any week. Choose your home class and your first class date when you register.
  • 8 classes at a time. Your first 8 classes are paid up front ($201.12 + HST), then you pay for each block of 8. I’ll send a reminder before each payment.
  • Move between classes. Thursday one week, Friday the next, space permitting.
  • Make-ups within the session, space permitting.
  • You only pay for classes that run. Classes pause over holiday breaks and carry over into the next session with no need to re-register.
  • Stop any time after your first 8 classes by emailing me before your next block begins.

Good to know

The practical details, in plain language.

Is there a registration fee?
No registration fee and no materials fee. The price you see, plus HST, is the price you pay. The one exception is a $35 + HST administrative fee if you withdraw after registering.
What’s the difference between a session and the membership?
A session is a set number of classes in one season, paid up front at $28 + HST a class, with two make-ups. The membership is $25.14 + HST a class, paid 8 classes at a time. You can join any week, move between Thursday and Friday, and keep going from session to session without re-registering.
How do make-up classes work?
Session families get 2 make-ups per session; members get unlimited make-ups within the session. Both are space permitting. Let us know before the class you’ll miss and we’ll find you another spot that week where we can.
Can I switch class times?
Yes, and there’s no fee. Switching to another time within the same age group and session is free, subject to space. Members can move between Thursday and Friday whenever they like.
Can I cancel my membership?
Yes. After your first 8 classes, just email contactmusicalsprouts@gmail.com before your next block of 8 begins, and you won’t be charged again. The $35 withdrawal fee doesn’t apply to memberships.
Can I spread out the cost?
The membership lets you pay 8 classes at a time. If you need a different arrangement for a session, just email me and we’ll work something out.
What if we join partway through a session?
Session fees are prorated from your first class, so you only pay for the weeks that are left. Drop us a note and we’ll send an adjusted invoice.
Which class does my child belong in?
Sproutlings is for toddlers 1–3 on Thursdays, Budding Sprouts for preschoolers 3–5 on Thursdays, and Sprouts All-Ages on Fridays welcomes 1–5 together. If your child is right on the edge, come to a trial or book a free call with Kelly and we’ll decide together.
Can visiting family come to a class?
Visiting family? Email me and I’ll let you know if there’s space.
Do you refund unused classes?
Session fees aren’t refundable once classes begin, and a $35 + HST administrative fee applies to withdrawals. In the case of a longer illness or a move we’ll issue a credit toward a future session.
